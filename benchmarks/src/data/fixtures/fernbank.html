Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Fernbank collects, uses, and retains personal data submitted during the recruitment process.

Data We Collect

We collect your CV, cover letter, interview notes, and assessment results when you apply for a role with us.

Data Retention

We retain candidate data for a period of 24 months following the conclusion of the recruitment process, after which it is securely deleted unless you have consented to be considered for future roles.

Sharing With Third Parties

We share limited candidate information with background-check and recruiting vendors who act on our behalf under data processing agreements.

Automated Screening

Fernbank uses AI-assisted screening tools to conduct an initial review of applications before they are seen by a human recruiter.

Data Protection Officer

Our Data Protection Officer can be contacted at dpo@fernbank.example with any questions about this notice.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your data at any time. To submit a data request, visit our Data Request Portal.
