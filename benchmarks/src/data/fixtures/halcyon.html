Data Protection Notice

Halcyon Systems takes data protection seriously. Some general information about DPO responsibilities across the industry is described below before we get to our own contact details — this paragraph deliberately mentions "DPO" early without an email address, to test that extraction doesn't stop at the first keyword match.

What We Collect

Name, email address, resume, and answers to screening questions.

Retention

Applicant records are kept for 12 months from the date of your last application, then permanently deleted from our systems.

Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes, and we do not sell candidate data under any circumstances.

Screening Process

All applications at Halcyon Systems are reviewed manually by our recruiting team. We do not use automated or AI-based screening tools at any stage of the hiring process.

Contact Our Data Protection Officer

For any privacy-related enquiries, please contact our appointed Data Protection Officer at privacy-officer@halcyonsystems.example.

Submitting a Request

Data subject requests can be submitted via our Data Rights Request Form.
